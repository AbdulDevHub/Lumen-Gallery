What Lumen Gallery does

  • Recent folders. Reopen any folder you've viewed in one click and pick up exactly where you stopped.
  • Library. Point it at a parent folder to see every sub-folder and jump between them with [ ].
  • Fast with huge folders. Thousands of images and GIFs scroll smoothly; images far off-screen are released from memory.
  • Flexible grid. 1, 2, 3, 6, 12 or 24 images per row, adjustable width, gaps on or off, natural or fill width.
  • Focused viewer. Auto sizing for tall and panoramic images, click zoom, a magnifying lens, a thumbnail strip and image info.
  • Hands-free. Slideshow with adjustable speed and smooth auto-scroll for long folders.
  • Organise while you browse. Favourites, filter by name, sort by name, date or size, or shuffle.
  • Videos too. Videos are turned into evenly spaced preview frames.
  • Private by design. Everything happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
  • Install anywhere. Use it as a website, install it as an offline app, or add the Chrome extension (Alt+G).

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Your browser reads the files directly from your device. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts and no analytics.

Does Lumen Gallery work offline?

Yes. Install it as an app from your browser's address bar, or use the Chrome extension, and it works without an internet connection.

How do I reopen a folder I viewed before?

Open a folder once with Open folder and it appears under Recent folders. Click it to reopen and continue from where you stopped. Your browser asks once for permission to read the folder.

Can it handle very large folders?

Yes. Folders with thousands of images stay smooth because only images near the screen are kept in memory, and GIFs only play when you hover them (you can change this in Settings).

Which file formats are supported?

Any image your browser can display, including JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, BMP and SVG. Videos such as MP4 and WebM are shown as evenly spaced preview frames.

Which browsers are supported?

All modern browsers. Recent folders and the Library use the File System Access API, available in Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave and other Chromium-based browsers.
